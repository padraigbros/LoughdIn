Privacy

Last updated 15 September 2026

Lough'd In is a personal focus app for tasks, focus sessions and planning. This page explains what it stores, where, and why. There are no adverts, analytics or tracking scripts.

Using it without an account

Your tasks, time blocks, focus sessions and settings are stored only in your browser or on your phone. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing the site's data or uninstalling the app removes them, so use Backup to export a copy if you want one.

Using it with an account

When you sign in, your data is kept in sync across your devices using Supabase, which hosts the account service and database in its London (eu-west-2) region. We store:

  • your email address and sign-in details, to identify your account;
  • your tasks, time blocks and completed focus sessions;
  • a record of recent changes and of the devices you use, so edits made offline or on two devices can be merged safely.

Each account's data can only be read and changed by that account. A timer that is running is not synced.

Signing in with Google

If you choose Sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with the app so it can create or find your account. We do not access your contacts, calendar, Drive or any other Google data. Google's handling of your information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Music

The optional music player plays internet radio directly from third-party stations (I Love Music, SomaFM and Musopen). When you press play, your device connects to that station, which can see your IP address. Nothing is sent to them unless you play a station.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders and session alerts are created on your device. They are not sent through a server.

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays until you delete it. Account data stays until you delete it or ask us to delete your account.

Deleting your data or account

You can delete tasks at any time in the app. To delete your account and everything synced with it, email padraigbrosnan@gmail.com from the address you signed in with. We will delete it and confirm by reply.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change with it.
